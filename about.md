  <section class="section grid2">
    <div class="prose">
      <p>I am a cognitive neuroscientist with more than ten years of experience studying how the brain keeps similar memories distinct. My work spans rodents and humans and combines behaviour, pharmacology, viral tools, imaging, electrophysiology and machine learning to link molecules, neural circuits and cognition, from basic mechanisms to translational questions about ageing.</p>
      <p>I trained at the University of Buenos Aires, worked as a postdoctoral researcher in Buenos Aires and, from 2021 to 2025, at the Institute of Functional Genomics in Montpellier. I have been awarded fellowships from the Human Frontier Science Program and the Fyssen Foundation.</p>
      <p>Position awarded in the 2025 CONICET career call; appointment pending national budget approval.</p>
